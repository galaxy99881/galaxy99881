- **Research code**: organizing experiments, papers, and reproducible implementations

## Featured Projects

**Unwatered does not mean intelligent. 不掺水，不等于智商高。**

## Operating Principle

> Fluent output is not proof of intelligence.  
> 流畅输出，不等于真实推理。

I prefer small tests that leave evidence behind:

- raw logs over vague impressions
- reproducible prompts over one-off screenshots
- simple tools over overbuilt systems
- practical failures over polished claims
